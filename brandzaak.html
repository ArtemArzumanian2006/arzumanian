DESCRIPTION

At Dapper I designed ads for BrandZaak, a Rotterdam coffee roastery, bringing their coffee collections to life with chalk, warmth and taste.

THE CHALLENGE

Next to Swissquote, I designed for BrandZaak, a coffee roastery that sells freshly roasted beans online to coffee lovers and businesses. The task was to promote their coffee collections with ads for Instagram, LinkedIn and their website. Every ad had to feel as warm and crafted as the coffee itself, and lead people to one action: taste, take the quiz or order.

CONTEXT

DOOR HET VUUR VOOR KOFFIE

"Through fire for coffee." BrandZaak was started in Rotterdam in 2013 by two brothers who wanted the whole country drinking fresh coffee. They roast every day, pack the beans the same day and ship them straight to people's homes and offices.

CAMPAIGNS

STORIES THAT STOP THE THUMB

A big part of the work was Instagram stories: full screen and seen for only a few seconds. I designed them for three campaigns, the Roasters Pick, the Single Origins Collection and an organic Guatemala highlight, together with the content managers who wrote the copy. Keep scrolling.

STYLE

CHALK ON THE BOARD

BrandZaak's website is built on a dark chalkboard with hand-drawn doodles, like the menu board in a cosy coffee bar. I carried that into the ads: chalk swirls, sketched beans and an espresso machine drawn line by line, so every ad feels like it comes from the same warm, in-house place.

PHOTOGRAPHY

BEANS IN THE FOREGROUND

To make flat product shots feel cinematic, I placed coffee beans at different depths. The closest ones are big and out of focus, the ones further back are small and sharp, and the bags sit right in the focus plane. It adds depth and pulls the eye straight to the product.

  • Close

    Big and blurred, like it's right in front of the lens.

  • In focus

    The product, sharp enough to read the label.

  • Further back

    Smaller beans that give the dark background some depth.

CONCEPT

A SECRET IN THE BAG

Sometimes the collection came with a surprise: a special coffee you could win, and the taste kept changing. To show it without spoiling it, I covered the bag's label with the beans themselves and added a chalk note. The copy tells you there's something special, the bag keeps the secret.

No label, no spoilers

CAMPAIGNS

EVERY TASTE ITS OWN COLOUR

For the Single Origins Collection every coffee got its own ad. The colour of each bag's label leads the design, so every taste feels different before you've read a word. Pick an origin.

Brasil

Sweet and balanced, with caramel and milk chocolate.

DESIGN

ROUND BY ROUND

The origin ads went through several rounds. The first put the bag on a gold pedestal. Then I pulled the label out as a callout so the taste notes were readable, and in the final round a block in the label's colour behind the bag made every ad recognisable at a glance.

FORMATS

ONE IDEA, EVERY FORMAT

Every design was made for stories and the feed, and adapted for LinkedIn and the website. This is the coffee quiz, which matches people with their perfect coffee in under 30 seconds.

OVERVIEW

THE COLLECTION

24 ads across five campaigns. Click any of them to see it up close.

REFLECTION

MEASURING SUCCESS

"Designing for a coffee brand taught me to design for a feeling. Nobody can taste an ad, so the chalk, the warm light and the beans had to do it for them. Working inside an existing brand style while still giving every campaign its own character was the real challenge, and the part I enjoyed most."
