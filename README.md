## About the Portfolio

This portfolio is a face of my growth and proficiency in designing and developing media products. The website serves as a platform for presenting my third-semester projects and learning outcomes to my assessors.

The primary objectives of this portfolio are to:
- Showcase the **Learning Outcomes (LOs)** I have mastered.
- Provide detailed documentation of projects I have been a part of.
- Demonstrate my skills in user-centered design, creative iterations, and professional communication.

---

## Learning Outcomes

The portfolio is designed around the following **Learning Outcomes (LOs):**

### **LO1 - Conceptualize, Design, and Develop Interactive Media Products**
- I brainstorm then create concept solutions that I transform into interactive media products using user-centered design principles, visual design techniques, and staying updated with emerging trends in media, design, and technology.

### **LO2 - Transferable Production**
- I document and comment my code clearly.
- I use **version control tools** in both personal and team contexts.
- I communicate technical recommendations to other developers.

### **LO3 - Creative Iterations**
- I present successive iterations of my creative process and explain the connections between them.

### **LO4 - Professional Standards**
- I apply professional methodologies to formulate project goals, involve stakeholders, conduct research, and deliver reports.
- I am able to work in a group with people of different cultural, ethical and linguistical backgrounds.

### **LO5 - Personal Leadership**
- I reflect on my strengths and weaknesses in ICT and personal development.
- I take actions aligned with my core values to promote personal growth and a learning-oriented attitude.
- I stay proactive in trying new media, ICT and professional related actions.
