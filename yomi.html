DESCRIPTION

With a teammate I created Yomi, a soft drink brand with a mystery twist. I designed the cans, built the 3D model and coded the website.

THE CHALLENGE

In a two-week school project, my teammate and I had to invent our own soft drink brand. Our twist was a mystery flavour: the label hides a clue that only shows up once the can is empty. Collect them all and win prizes, from Olympic tickets to front-row concert seats.

RESEARCH

TAKING NOTES

We started by collecting can designs we liked and pinning what was worth exploring in our own: bold type, big juicy fruit and playful colour.

RESEARCH

TWO PEOPLE, ONE CAN

The brief's twist: please both the people who love a surprise and the ones who want to know what they're drinking. We built the brand around two personas, Bram (22) and Sem (10).

RESEARCH

SETTING THE MOOD

Ice, fresh fruit, water and running tracks: cold, fresh and sporty. The moodboard also set a bold header font and a bright palette.

PACKAGING

QUANTITY OVER QUALITY

Instead of aiming for the perfect label straight away, I designed fast and a lot: strawberry first, then orange, then the Olympic edition. Every version fixed something from the last. Here is a selection, straight from Figma.

PACKAGING

THE FINAL FLAVOURS

Strawberry, orange, kiwi and blueberry. One hero fruit per can, the same layout throughout, and a back with nutrition facts and a QR code to join the campaign. Pick a flavour.

Strawberry

PACKAGING

WHAT'S UNDER THE RINGS

In the Olympic edition the flavour itself is the mystery. While the can is full and cold, the box shows the Olympic rings. Finish it and a flag-coloured QR code appears. Collect every flag to win 2 tickets to Milano Cortina 2026.

Flip the switch to finish the can.

3D

FROM FLAT TO 3D

Starting from a free can model, I mapped my own label onto it in Blender, removed the leftover embossing from the original brand and exported it for the web.

Drag to spin
DEVELOPMENT

ONE SIP CLOSER

From paper sketches through eight Figma versions to a coded one-pager. One scroll animation moves and tilts the 3D can from section to section, and you can flick it to make it spin.

CAMPAIGN

FUEL THE CHASE

Three posters for the campaign: sweat, speed and fresh fruit, with a can of Yomi at the centre of each.

REFLECTION

MEASURING SUCCESS

"Two weeks is not much time to build a brand, so I picked speed over perfection: design, look, fix, repeat. Making many versions early taught me more than polishing one, and taking the same label from a flat file to a 3D can on a live website showed me how far one idea can travel."
