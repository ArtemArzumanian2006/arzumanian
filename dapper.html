DESCRIPTION

As an in-house designer I also designed for Dapper itself: an award entry, office signs, merch and an internal campaign.

THE CHALLENGE

Next to client work like Swissquote and BrandZaak, I designed for Dapper itself, the B2B demand agency in Rotterdam I work for. That meant a bit of everything: an award entry, signs for the office, merch and an internal campaign. Each piece had to feel like Dapper: bold, playful and never boring.

AWARD ENTRY

NOW STREAMING: B2B

Dapper entered an award for successful LinkedIn campaigns with a series my colleagues made: famous scenes from Pulp Fiction, Suits and The Wolf of Wall Street, rewritten as B2B marketing stories. I got a lot of copy from our content marketers and one big banner to fit it all in. My idea: make it look like Netflix, a screen everyone already knows how to read.

  1. The side menu

    Netflix's menu holds the background and the idea, so the long copy reads like browsing, not like a report.

  2. The show

    Pulp Fiction gets a real title treatment, a match score and a season tag. The description tells how it was made.

  3. The results

    1M+ impressions per video and double the inbound leads, big enough to read from across the room.

  4. The execution

    How the series was made, released and spread, split into three short columns.

  5. Your next watch

    The other two recreations, Suits and The Wolf of Wall Street, lined up as the next shows.

OFFICE

MEET THE ROOMS

The team voted on movie names for the meeting rooms, each with "meet" worked into the title. I turned them into door signs that borrow the look of each film's logo, so you get the reference before you've read a word. Simple and white, they're now on the office doors, together with a sign for our podcast and photo studio.

MERCH

MERCH YOU'D WEAR OUTSIDE

Management wanted merch that's more street style: something people want to wear outside the office and that makes others ask about the company. I came up with the direction and designed the full set: the Dapper plant in the centre, like Iron Man's arc reactor, with wireframe graphics around it.

CAMPAIGN

PEAK PERFORMANCE WEEK

A week at Dapper about sharp habits: early to bed, healthy food, no cheap dopamine and a clean desk. I designed the poster series for the office and a set of phone wallpapers for the team. Sports photography, huge type and short lines to push you through the week.

MEASURING SUCCESS

"Designing for your own company is a different kind of pressure: your colleagues are the client, and they walk past your work every day. It taught me to make things that are clear at a glance but still hide a detail that makes people smile, like a door sign that's also a movie reference."
